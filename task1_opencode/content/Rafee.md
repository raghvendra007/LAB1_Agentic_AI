## What I think about AI in software engineering today

From a fresh developer perspective, AI is an unbelievable interactive tutor that demystifies obscure compiler errors in seconds. But relying on it too early is a trap: it creates an illusion of competence where you understand the output without understanding the underlying mechanics.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years

Natural language and intent-driven wireframes will directly compile into accessible, responsive user interfaces. The frontend engineer's job will focus almost entirely on user empathy, accessibility, and high-performance client-side state rather than CSS layout fighting.

## My hot take on AI

If you can't debug code without an LLM, you are the copilot, not the agent.
