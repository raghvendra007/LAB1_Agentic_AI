## What I think about AI in software engineering today

AI assistants are fantastic productivity multipliers for well-understood domains and boilerplate generation, but they break down when faced with complex legacy enterprise codebases. They often exhibit blind confidence on subtle synchronization primitives and concurrency issues. Right now, AI acts as a fast copilot, but you cannot let it navigate without senior oversight.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years

Engineering will center heavily on systems integration, prompt-driven formal verification, and distributed consensus rather than mundane feature programming. Junior roles will evolve into system orchestration positions where knowing failure modes and domain invariants will matter far more than knowing low-level library APIs.

## My hot take on AI

AI autocomplete hasn't eliminated bugs; it has just made them compile much faster.
