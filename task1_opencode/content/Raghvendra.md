## What I think about AI in software engineering today

Current LLMs are incredible for rapid prototyping and translating business logic into SQL or boilerplate endpoints. However, they lack deep situational context and often pull in deprecated methods without warning. Their true utility is reducing initial friction, not making core architectural choices.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years

Developers will primarily converse with multi-agent pipelines that autonomously build, test, and benchmark microservices against synthetic workloads. Humans will intervene primarily to define objective functions, enforce privacy boundaries, and resolve conflicting business constraints.

## My hot take on AI

90% of current 'AI agent' workflows are just fragile `while` loops wrapped around regex parsers.
