## What I think about AI in software engineering today

AI brings creativity and swift prototyping into engineering, making the barrier to entry much lower for novel ideas. However, software is as much about human communication and interface empathy as it is about syntax. Current models produce functional code, but it often lacks aesthetic intention and elegance.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years

Software design will become much more human-centric and multidisciplinary. Engineers will guide agents using high-level design systems and ethical standards, treating code generation as a utility while spending their creative energy on user delight and seamless interactions.

## My hot take on AI

Prompt engineering is not the future of software engineering; thoughtful human design is.
